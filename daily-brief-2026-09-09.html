AI enters the scientific feedback loop

Eighteen consequential developments across six standing beats, stack-ranked for the September 6–9 decision window.

Recursive research acceleration is no longer hypothetical. OpenAI says coding agents now deliver 3.1 agent-workdays per human researcher day, while its chief scientist warns monitoring and alignment may not sustain maximum-speed scaling. Governments are answering with operational controls: Australia proposes a duty of care covering chatbots, Europe has begun active AI Act supervision, and China’s top court has drawn liability lines around deepfakes, cloning and privacy. Mistral’s €3 billion raise and China’s AI-focused IP plan show sovereign capability and governance advancing together. The strategic question is shifting from who builds the strongest model to who can prove accelerated capability remains observable, governable and legally deployable.
