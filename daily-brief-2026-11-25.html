AI control moves from principle to permission

Eighteen consequential developments across six standing beats, stack-ranked for the November 22–25 decision window.

AI sovereignty is becoming an evaluation-and-standards contest, not only a frontier-model race. A proposed UN framework and practical UK sandbox point toward interoperable evidence for market access, while China is exporting influence through open weights, data standards and a proven domestic compute stack. GPT-6.2 crosses declared cyber and self-improvement thresholds as verified agent research produces formal scientific gains. Privacy regulators are enforcing the same principle: control must be explicit, testable and off by default—not merely promised.
