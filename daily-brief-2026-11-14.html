Compute, capital and control converge

Nineteen consequential developments across six standing beats, stack-ranked for the November 11–14 decision window.

The frontier race is becoming an infrastructure-and-control contest. Anthropic’s disclosures put extraordinary compute obligations and strategic financing in view, while Google and OpenAI turn capable agents into faster, governed products. China is answering chip constraints with coordinated data-centre, robotics and full-stack investment. Europe’s privacy penalties and sovereign-AI agenda show that scale without auditable consent and governance carries material cost. Leaders should treat compute concentration, transaction authority and provable controls as one connected operating risk.
