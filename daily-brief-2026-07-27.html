The rulebook becomes an operating system

Twelve consequential developments across six standing beats, stack-ranked for the July 23–27 decision window.

Executive summary

Europe’s AI rulebook moved from legislative text to operating reality. The AI Omnibus entered into force today, extending selected high-risk timelines and widening sandbox access while preserving the August 2 transparency milestone. The signal is sequencing, not deregulation: inventories, labels, provenance and accountable ownership must ship before the more complex conformity regime.

Frontier competition is becoming a contest over who sets the access model. Lab leaders are calling for independent evaluations before AGI, OpenAI is tying frontier capacity to national science, and China is pairing cheap open models with an inclusion-led governance pitch. Leaders should connect compliance evidence, evaluation security and model-supply-chain strategy now.
